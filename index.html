Our Products

01

Agriculture

Chemical Fertilizers Trading

NTA Group sources and supplies high-grade mineral fertilizers that raise yields and replenish soils for farms and agri-businesses worldwide. We connect leading producers with distributors, cooperatives and government buyers, handling contract negotiation, quality assurance and bulk or bagged delivery end to end.

  • Nitrogen: urea, UAN, ammonium nitrate and CAN
  • Phosphates: DAP, MAP, TSP and rock phosphate
  • Potash: MOP and SOP for potassium-demanding crops
  • NPK blends and specialty water-soluble fertilizers
  • Bulk vessel and bagged (50 kg / jumbo) supply options
02

Energy

Industrial & Liquefied Natural Gas Trading

We trade liquefied natural gas and industrial gases as a cleaner-burning energy source for power generation, heating and manufacturing. Backed by strict safe-handling protocols and dependable marine and overland logistics, we link gas producers and terminals with industrial and utility offtakers.

  • Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG): spot and term cargoes
  • Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG): propane and butane
  • Industrial gases for manufacturing and processing
  • Cryogenic carrier and ISO-tank logistics
  • Regasification and terminal coordination
03

Energy

Trading Refined Oil Products Abroad

NTA Group trades the full slate of refined petroleum products, moving fuels from refineries to the markets that depend on them. With secure contracts, independent inspection at every stage and flexible shipping, we keep transport, industry and power dependably supplied.

  • Gasoline (PMS) and gasoil / diesel (AGO)
  • Jet fuel (Jet A-1) and kerosene
  • Fuel oil (HFO / LSFO) and bitumen
  • Base oils and lubricants
  • Certified to international product specifications
04

Energy

Crude Oil Trading Abroad

We source and supply crude oil grades on the international market, connecting producers and refiners through secure contracts and efficient shipping. Our trading desk manages price exposure, documentation and vessel scheduling from origin to discharge.

  • Light, medium and heavy crude grades
  • Sweet and sour crude streams
  • FOB and CIF delivery terms
  • Tanker chartering and voyage management
  • SGS / independent cargo inspection
05

Industry

Petrochemicals Trading

NTA Group trades a broad range of petrochemical products and feedstocks that form the building blocks of modern manufacturing, from plastics and packaging to textiles and coatings. We connect producers with industrial buyers across global markets.

  • Polymers: polyethylene (PE), polypropylene (PP), PVC
  • Aromatics: benzene, toluene and xylene
  • Olefins: ethylene and propylene
  • Methanol, MEG and industrial solvents
  • Bulk, FCL and ISO-tank supply
06

Agriculture

Grains, Cereals & Legumes Trading

We supply grains, cereals and legumes that underpin food security and industry, sourcing dependable volumes at consistent quality. From staple cereals to protein-rich pulses, we serve millers, processors, importers and government reserves.

  • Wheat, maize (corn) and barley
  • Rice: long-grain, parboiled and basmati
  • Soybeans and soybean meal
  • Pulses: lentils, chickpeas, beans and peas
  • Bulk vessel and containerised, quality inspected
