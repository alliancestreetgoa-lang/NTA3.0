About NTA Group

A trusted name in international commodity trading

Headquartered in the United Arab Emirates, NTA Group operates at the crossroads of global trade. We source, supply and distribute essential commodities — from chemical fertilizers and grains that sustain agriculture, to crude oil, refined products, natural gas and petrochemicals that power industry.

Built on transparency, compliance and long-term relationships, we serve producers, refiners, distributors and governments with dependable supply, competitive pricing and end-to-end logistics.
