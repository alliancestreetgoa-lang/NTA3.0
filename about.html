About NTA Group

A UAE-based international trading house connecting global resources with the markets that need them.

Who We Are

Trade built on trust

NTA Group operates at the crossroads of global trade, sourcing and supplying essential commodities across energy and agriculture. From the United Arab Emirates — one of the world's premier trade gateways — we link producers, refiners, distributors and governments worldwide.

Our diversified portfolio spans six core commodity lines: chemical fertilizers, industrial & liquefied natural gas, refined oil products, crude oil, petrochemicals, and grains, cereals & legumes. This breadth lets us serve partners across the full value chain — from the fields that feed nations to the fuels and feedstocks that power industry.

We believe lasting trade is built on transparency, compliance and relationships that endure. Every shipment is handled with rigorous quality control and end-to-end logistics, so our partners can plan with confidence.

Our Mission

To supply essential energy and agricultural commodities reliably and responsibly — creating dependable value for our partners and the communities and economies they serve.

Our Vision

To be a globally trusted UAE trading house recognised for integrity, quality and the seamless movement of resources between the world's producing and consuming markets.
